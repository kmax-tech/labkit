# Classifying the checks per cause — a sample of 21, 2026-11-11

*A test of the categories, not a census: the sample was chosen for its hard cases and says nothing about how often a problem occurs among the 278 finding sites. The ordering session read each row at the code; one reader, not checked independently. Follows [the review of the rule assignments](2026-11-11-rule-assignment-review.md), whose round on the reasons is closed.*

## The question

The author wants every concrete cause of a check classified in four dimensions: what is checked (subject), on which basis (lab rule, technical contract, unsettled), by which procedure (exact, static hint, audit of content), and with which outcome and effect — the two apart, since "check not executable" and "blocks" can hold together.

Three of the four are already data on every gate finding (`labkit/formats/gate.py`), and the inventory lists two of them per site (`python -m labkit.tools.check_inventory`):

| Dimension | Field                       | Values                                                     | In the inventory |
|-----------|-----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|
| basis     | `GateFinding.rule`          | known rule, technical contract, open assignment, new class | yes              |
| procedure | `GateFinding.evidence_kind` | guaranteed, static evidence                                | yes              |
| outcome   | `GateFinding.check_outcome` | violated, not executable                                   | no               |
| effect    | `GateFinding.grade`         | BLOCKER, WARNING                                           | yes              |

So the sample is a check of these fields, not a second classification beside them. New in the table are the subject and the cause; the source is named by function, which stays when lines move.

## What the sample shows

The four dimensions are enough to tell these 21 causes apart. Fifteen rows fit or fit in part. The gaps are these, each with the row that shows it:

1. **The outcome has no value for a suspicion** (row 17). A static hint records "violated" by default. Either the outcome gets a third value, or it is defined for exact checks only and the procedure says the rest. The agent audit already has the word (`undetermined`, row 20).
2. **The outcome is not in the inventory** (rows 5, 6, 12). 16 sites set it; where it is computed from an error, only a run can show it.
3. **The basis holds one value** (row 14). Two carrying rules are recorded as open. That is a limit of the field, not a missing reason; whether the field takes several rules is undecided.
4. **"Violated" with an unsettled basis** (rows 2, 16). The check is exact and its condition holds, but no decided duty is breached. Outcome and basis are independent, and the word "violated" hides it. A static-evidence blocker is refused by the gate; a blocker with an open basis is allowed, and nothing says whether that is meant.
5. **Three kinds of finding lie outside `GateFinding`** (rows 19, 20, 21): the report review's claim, the agent audit's finding, and what is printed or refused without a finding. The first two are the audit-of-content procedure; a gate's check that a valid review exists (row 18) is a different cause with a different procedure, and the table keeps them apart.
6. **"Information" as an effect exists only for the audit** (NOTE, row 20). A gate's note is printed and is no finding.
7. **The unit "cause" is only partly in the inventory.** A site whose rule the cause passes in shows "variable" (rows 7, 13); the causes are listed in the inventory's last table for the open ones only.
8. **Two sites are wrong or doubtful as recorded** (rows 5, 8) — found by reading the row, not by the categories.

The subject took seven values in 21 rows: protocol text or head, generated line, code against the registration, repository or installation state, a record against the state, report prose, content. Whether that list holds is the first question for the full pass.

## Not decided here

- whether the outcome gets a value for a suspicion, or is limited to exact checks (gap 1);
- whether the rule field takes more than one rule (gap 3);
- whether a blocker may stand on an unsettled basis, and how the outcome is worded then (gap 4);
- whether the review's claim and the printed warnings get the fields, or stay outside by intent (gap 5).

Nothing is changed in the code by the sample itself; what followed is below.

## Decided 2026-11-11, and a proposal

The author's decisions on the gaps: a suspicion is an outcome of its own (gap 1); several rules are possible in principle where one indivisible condition touches several duties, not where several causes are printed together, and the field's readers are listed before a format change (gap 3); a blocker on an unsettled basis stays for now and is decided one by one, "violated" is too strong there, and the open basis stays visible (gap 4); the kinds of finding stay apart, the classification shows them together with fields marked not applicable (gap 5); the inventory tells a value fixed in the source from one computed at run time (gap 2); the two sites of gap 8 are corrected.

**The two sites.** The missing pytest records "not executable" and its line says so (1cfe744e). The registered id without a key literal is **not changed, and row 8 above is corrected here**: the author's condition for a hint was that keys assembled at run time are allowed, and they are not. The message says so ("a key assembled at run time is not seen, and blocks here too"), LAB-018 Routine check after every implementation describes the check as "Every identifier appears as a key literal in the scripts", and CODE-016 The experiment area reconstructs itself states it as a duty: "Every registered key is set in the area script", as literal strings. For that duty the check is exact. What does not fit is the recorded rule: LAB-004 states the contract on `metrics.json`, for which a literal in the source is an indication. The open question is the assignment (CODE-016 by wording, or LAB-018), the author's; it was step 2 of the earlier order.

**Proposal: outcome values.** Two values today, `violated` and `not executable`; 10 lines of `labkit/formats/gate.py` read the field, no other module does.

| Outcome          | Says                                                                                                                  | Holds when                                                                   |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `violated`       | an exact check found a known rule or a technical contract breached                                                    | guaranteed, basis a known rule or a technical contract                       |
| `deviation`      | the checked deviation was found; its binding basis is not settled — the duty may be undecided, or only its assignment | guaranteed, basis an open assignment, a new class, or no assignment recorded |
| `suspected`      | an indication was found; a breach of duty is not established                                                          | static evidence, any basis                                                   |
| `not executable` | the check could not run here; it comes before every other outcome                                                     | any procedure, stated by the site                                            |

Both new values follow from fields a finding already has (the procedure, the kind of basis). So the smaller build stores nothing new: one function in `gate.py` gives the outcome from the three fields, the line and the inventory read it, and no site is edited (17 static-evidence sites and 16 open sites today). Storing the value at each site would let the three fields disagree. Recommended: derive it. The words `deviation` and `suspected` are a suggestion; the name is the author's.

Built 2026-11-11 as decided by the author: `gate.derived_outcome` is the one derivation and `GateFinding.outcome` what every reader takes. A site states only `not executable` (`GateFinding.stated_outcome`, until this build `check_outcome`, `None` by default, so a site that states nothing is told from one that states `violated`); a stated value that contradicts the derived one raises, and so does an evidence kind or a rule kind the derivation does not name. The one site that stated `violated` on an open assignment (`evidence.change_blockers`) states nothing now and reads `deviation`. A failed read states `not executable` or nothing (`gate.outcome_of_failed_read`). Two readings of this build, not decided by the author: a new class and a site without a recorded assignment read `deviation`, since neither names a decided basis; and no line changed — a line still names only a check not executed, so `deviation` and `suspected` are data for the inventory and for a count, not words a reader of a gate sees.

**Proposal: several rules.** No format change now. The one case found (the registered comparison changed together with a field) is two causes printed in one hold, not one indivisible condition: by the author's own criterion each cause carries its assignment. The build is two findings from `seen_hold` and `now_hold`, the comparison under LAB-035 and the fields under LAB-003, each with its own way out. The rule field is read in nine modules (`gate`, `findings`, `check_inventory`, `answering_run`, `preflight`, `promises`, `followup`, `report_blocks`, `intent_check`); a field of several rules waits for a condition that cannot be split.

Built 2026-11-11: `registration_hold.by_cause` parts the differences, both holds tell one BLOCKER per cause, and `scope_rule` refuses the comparison beside another part instead of recording an open assignment. A hold of one cause is unchanged, byte for byte (five cases compared against the previous module). One behaviour follows from the split and was not asked for by name: a field whose correction is decided leads its own finding with the follow-up audit, where the joint hold led with the restore because the comparison has no correction.

**Proposal: the inventory.** One more column, the outcome: the value where the site writes a constant, `computed` where it calls `outcome_of_failed_read`, and the derived value otherwise.

Limits of the classification that stay as they are: the review's claim, the audit's finding and what is printed or refused without a finding keep their own types; a gate's note is no finding; the unit "cause" is listed only where a site receives its rule.

## What carries an assignment — the measure widened, 2026-11-11

*Decided by the author on 2026-11-11, after the second pass. The table below is the ordering session's reading, one reader, not checked independently; no code is changed by it.*

Until now a rule carried a finding only where one sentence of its text names exactly the checked duty. The first pass (91 sites) and the second pass (per cause) judged by that measure. It was too narrow: a rule does not have to list every technical case. From now on three kinds of support carry an assignment:

- **wording** — a sentence of the rule states the duty;
- **reference** — the rule points to another rule or procedure by name, and that text states the duty;
- **inference** — the duty follows from the rule's text by one step that needs no further methodical decision. The step is written down.

What still does not carry: nearness of topic, and "it would be sound science". A sentence that describes what a tool does is read at its site; a yes at one site is no yes for the next. Verdicts of the two earlier passes stand as they were judged, by the narrow measure. They are read again only where this table does it, and nothing is closed in bulk.

## Terms

- **Finding site:** a place the inventory lists; in these two modules all 65 are gate findings, none an exit or a printed line.
- **Condition:** what is compared with what, on which input, for the site to tell its finding.
- **Asserted:** a test checks a constant part of the site's own sentence (not only its grade, its rule, or words another function passed in). "Partly" says which part is missing.
- **Clear from the code:** the site's function and functions of the same module decide the condition. **Needed callers:** the deciding comparison or its input stands in another module, which was opened. **Unsure:** the deciding code was not opened, or a doubt remains; the reason is given.

## 3. A duties table, judged on these two modules

### What one row would have to carry

`Lane` works because each field is one decision with one value per repeat cost (`labkit/formats/standard_contract.py:74`). The rows above need more than that:

1. the condition as a sentence, and the function that decides it (often not the function that tells it);
2. the input it is decided on (the run's record, the tree, git, the first measurements' texts);
3. the rule assignment and the grade, each as a value or as "by cause";
4. the sites that tell it, and the callers that tell one site;
5. when it is due: lane, workflow, with or without an answering run, and what must have passed before;
6. what it yields to, so that one cause is told once;
7. the test that holds the condition and its valid twin.

### Rows that fit without loss

A row fits when one site holds one condition with one rule and one grade, and the deciding code is the site's own. Of the 65, 36 fit on those terms: 27 that record a rule or an open assignment and 9 failed reads told once. The 27: `labkit/gates/registration_hold.py:234`, `:420`, `:446`; `labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:242`, `:255`, `:266`, `:549`, `:675`, `:817`, `:853`, `:951`, `:1039`, `:1199`, `:1364`, `:1385`, `:1394`, `:1423`, `:1572`, `:1691`, `:1746`, `:1808`, `:1868`, `:1879`, `:1919`, and the three comparisons of the code duty (`:408`, `:513`, `:596`) as one row with three sites. The 9: `labkit/gates/registration_hold.py:943`, `:995`; `labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:199`, `:573`, `:783`, `:1157`, `:1193`, `:1613`, `:1735`. The count depends on the terms: "due at low repeat cost" for six of them still stands outside the site.

### Rows that do not fit, and why

1. **One site, several conditions.** `labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:1143` holds three causes with two rule assignments and two outcomes; `:745` holds two conditions under one sentence slot; `:396` and `:348` each hold "gone" and "ambiguous"; `:550` names whichever of two causes holds. A row per site hides conditions; a row per condition has no site of its own to point at, only a branch.
2. **One comparison, the rule chosen by what differs.** `labkit/gates/registration_hold.py:258` records LAB-003 or an open assignment by whether a declared scope changed (`:151`); `:771`, `:806` and `:920` record LAB-003, LAB-035 or an open assignment per cause (`:124`). The rule is a function of the difference found, not a property of the duty.
3. **The condition is decided elsewhere.** `labkit/gates/registration_hold.py:1025`, `:1062`, `:1100` and `:1158` test whether a tuple is empty; the comparison is in `labkit/formats/area_state.py:555` and `:1032`. The row would name `area_state` as the holder, and the site as its teller only.
4. **The duty is an order, not a condition.** The four holds on an untold first measurement apply "the first that applies" (`labkit/gates/registration_hold.py:171`); `source_finding` decides gone, then untracked, then drifted bytes, and does not hash a source the earlier step decided (`labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:945`, `:924`); the scope hold compares the run's text first and the standing text only when that found nothing (`labkit/gates/registration_hold.py:607`, `:629`). Independent rows lose the precedence, which is part of what is told.
5. **Grade and consequence follow the caller.** The same blockers of `code_since_run` are a finding in `finalize` (`labkit/gates/finalize.py:545`), an exit in the recomputation (`labkit/checks/recheck.py:436`), and only a yes or no for the way out of the scope hold (`labkit/gates/registration_hold.py:641`).
6. **Conditions that are no site.** A module in another repository is a string here (`labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:525`) and becomes a warning in `finalize` (`labkit/gates/finalize.py:549`); the notes on unstamped rows and on sources kept outside git are conditions with no finding at all (`labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:1767`, `:962`). A table built from the site list misses them.
7. **Failed reads told three times.** The broken lineage (`labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:182`, `:1683`, `:1722`) is one precondition with two grades at three stations. One row with three sites is possible; it then states a grade per site, which is the thing the table was meant to hold once.

### What a test could hold against the code, and what stays reading

Mechanical, in the manner of `tests/test_check_overview.py`:

- every site of the inventory is named by exactly one row or is listed as a teller of one, and every row names a site that exists (by function and words; line numbers move);
- a row's grade, evidence kind and rule assignment equal what the site records, where the site states them as constants: 60 of the 65 here, the 5 variable ones not;
- the rows named as tests exist and carry the mark `rule`;
- a row's sites with a passed-in sentence (`labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:199`, `:348`, `:396`, `:745`, `:839`, `:1735`) are flagged, since the scan cannot read their words.

Stays a human assignment:

- the condition sentence itself, and whether two sites hold the same condition (the nine groups above were found by reading, and several of them have unlike wording, for instance the lineage and the model record);
- which function decides, where the site only tells;
- when the duty is due and what it yields to: these stand as `if` conditions and comments at entry points (`labkit/gates/finalize.py:1327`, `labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:1667`, `:815`);
- whether a test asserts the condition or only reaches the site. Here 58 of 65 assert a part of the site's own sentence, but the search that showed it was by phrase per site, and it cannot tell an assertion on the condition from one on the wording.

### What would have to be decided

1. Whether a row is a condition or a site. The two modules have 65 sites and, by the reading above, 64 conditions: six fewer where sites share one (the script not found, code that does not read, the lineage, the changed code), five more where a site holds several (the output record, the denominator, the script sentence, the missing commit or repository). The counts are close; the mapping is not one to one.
2. Whether a failed read is a duty of the table or a property of each reader. It is 28 of the 65 sites: the 32 that record a technical contract less the four named in section 2.
3. Whether precedence among conditions belongs in the table.
4. Whether the rule of a comparison may depend on what differs, or whether such a site is two duties.

### Side observations, not followed up

1. `source_finding`'s docstring says the rule of a gone source is not decided (`labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:943`); `_gone` records the technical contract (`:1163`).
2. The module docstring says `finalize`'s stations decide the grade (`labkit/checks/run_record_check.py:14`); every site sets its grade itself and `finalize` takes the findings as they are at the calls read (`labkit/gates/finalize.py:384`, `:773`).
3. `scope_differences` also compares `[explored_in]` (`labkit/formats/protocol_history.py:479`). `registration_hold` names it nowhere (a search for `explored_in` in the module gave 0 hits, and found it in `protocol_history`), so by `scope_rule` a changed `[explored_in]` records LAB-003 (`labkit/gates/registration_hold.py:124`).

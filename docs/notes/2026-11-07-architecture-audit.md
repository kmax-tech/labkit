Three readers in parallel (check flow, layers and responsibilities, homes of logic); every finding below was opened at its lines, the reader reports stayed in the session scratchpad.

Verdict: the coarse structure holds — the downward import rule is tested and kept, `preflight_area` and `finalize_area` read as numbered flows with their skips at the call, and git, digests, the run id, JSONL and the lab root each have one home. The weak spots are three. (2) Helpers sit in the module of their first caller, which makes five import cycles and five modules with several jobs each. (3) Below the "One home per question" table, editing protocol text and reading a finding's place are spelled several times.

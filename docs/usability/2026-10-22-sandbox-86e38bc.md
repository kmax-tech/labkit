## The new step, as a cold agent met it

Only judge-2 accepted a BLOCKER with `stands` (the keyword-rule finding,
reason: C1 is registered as a threshold over the null, not as
recognition independent of connectives). It ran `labkit-audit --review`
on its own, from AGENTS.md step 5, before `--from-run`; the reviewer
upheld both decisions (0.37 USD nested). Then two gate refusals, both the
new step's:

1. The review had judged a placeholder Conclusion (the reviewer said so:
   "with the Conclusion still a placeholder, the claim is the Recorded
   results line alone"). The agent wrote the Conclusion afterwards, the
   claim's digest changed, the review went stale, and report-check
   blocked the adoption — correctly by the contract, wastefully for the
   person: a second review (0.5 USD) upheld the same two decisions under
   the written claim.
2. The blocker's sentence names the record as `--record
   2026-10-22T13-43-analysis.toml`; the agent copied it and was refused
   with `No such file or directory` — the name resolves only from
   `audits/`. It retried with the path and succeeded.

Both fixed on this state's successor: `--record` takes the bare name
(resolved under the area's `audits/`, for `--decide` and `--findings`
too), and `--review` refuses while the Conclusion is a placeholder,
naming why; AGENTS.md says "write the Conclusion, then review".

judge-1 and judge-3 accepted WARNINGs only, saw the "not reviewed"
warnings in `--from-run`'s output and reported; no review is owed there
yet. Their reasons are the round's usual readings (connective cues,
one draw, the coarse 9/12 threshold) — material for the day the WARNING
scope is decided.

# Plan: eeg-sub01-to-sub03

The plan for this task, written before any work on it. Follow it; where it cannot be followed, say so in REPORT.md.

- **Terms:** image: one of the 16,540 training images (200 test images), its signal 17 channels by 200 time points flattened to 3,400 values, channel-major (column c * 200 + t); normalisation: each subject's signal minus its mean, divided by its standard deviation, both one number over all values of that subject's training images — inside cross-validation both from the fitting fold's images only, for the deliverable from all training images; mapping: a ridge regression from Sub 01's normalised signal to Sub 03's for the same image; image correlation: the Pearson correlation between the predicted and the true 3,400 values of one image; mean response: the mean of Sub 03's normalised training signals of the fitting fold, the same 3,400 values for every image; deliverable: pred_results/linear_sub01tosub03_pred.npy, shape (200, 3400), rows in the order of Sub 01's test images, on the normalised scale of Sub 03's training data
- **Question / hypothesis:** Does a linear mapping from Sub 01's EEG to Sub 03's predict Sub 03's signal for held-out images better than the mean response does, measured as the mean out-of-fold image correlation over the training images? Expected: yes.
- **Inference criterion:** the mean out-of-fold image correlation is at least the mean response's mean out-of-fold image correlation plus margin = 0.05 (researcher's choice)
- **Quality check:** every training image has an out-of-fold prediction (max_empty = 0)
- **Data / scope:** benchmark/datasets/thingseeg2/train/sub01.npy, train/sub03.npy and test/sub01.npy as supplied, read as float32; the files are used as supplied, not copied or changed
- **Model / tool:** the estimator of `make_estimator()` in scripts/01_measure.py
- **Null condition / anchors:** the mean response, scored like the mapping in the same folds: the bar of the criterion. Its image correlation need not be 0 — images share signal structure, so a mean can correlate with each — and no band is set on it; the same mapping refitted on image pairings permuted inside each fitting fold is reported beside it, as a check that the pairing carries the signal, and blocks nothing
- **Exclusions / filters:** none
- **Unit:** one image; the kept number is the mean out-of-fold image correlation; the deliverable holds the 200 test images' predicted Sub 03 signals from the mapping fitted on all training images

Fixed numbers, set before any result: criterion: margin = 0.05; first check: max_empty = 0.

## Task (verbatim)

  ```text
  Train a linear model to learn the mapping of neural representations in EEG signals from one subject (Sub 01) to another (Sub 03) based on the preprocessed EEG data from Sub 01 and Sub 03. Then use the test set of Subject 1 (Sub 01) to generate EEG signal of Subject 3 (Sub 03). Save the generated EEG signal of Subject 3 to 'pred_results/linear_sub01tosub03_pred.npy'.

  Output format: `pred_results/linear_sub01tosub03_pred.npy`, a NumPy array of floats with shape (200, 3400). Row i holds the generated Sub 03 signal for test image i, in the order of the images in Sub 01's test set; column c * 200 + t holds channel c at time point t (17 channels, 200 time points each). Values are on the normalised scale of Sub 03's training data.
  ```

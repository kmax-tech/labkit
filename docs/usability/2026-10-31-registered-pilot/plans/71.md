# Plan: eeg-sub01-to-sub03

The plan for this task, written before any work on it. Follow it; where it cannot be followed, say so in REPORT.md.

- **Terms:** image: one of the 16,540 training images (200 test images), its signal 17 channels by 200 time points flattened to 3,400 values, channel-major (column c * 200 + t); mapping: a ridge regression from Sub 01's flattened signal to Sub 03's for the same image; image correlation: the Pearson correlation between the predicted and the true 3,400 values of one image; deliverable: pred_results/linear_sub01tosub03_pred.npy, shape (200, 3400), rows in the order of Sub 01's test images, on the normalised scale of Sub 03's training data
- **Question / hypothesis:** Does a linear mapping from Sub 01's EEG to Sub 03's predict Sub 03's signal for held-out images clearly better than an unfitted mapping, measured as the mean out-of-fold image correlation over the training images? Expected: yes.
- **Inference criterion:** the mean out-of-fold image correlation is at least the mean of the permuted-label refits plus margin = 0.2
- **Quality check:** every training image has an out-of-fold prediction (max_empty = 0)
- **Data / scope:** benchmark/datasets/thingseeg2/train/sub01.npy, train/sub03.npy and test/sub01.npy as supplied, read as float32; the files are used as supplied, not copied or changed
- **Model / tool:** n/a — no model call; the instrument is the estimator of `make_estimator()` in scripts/01_measure.py
- **Null condition / anchors:** the same estimator refitted on labels permuted inside each fitting fold, twice per fold; the mean of their scores is expected within null_band of chance (an image correlation of 0), and the criterion's bar starts from that mean
- **Exclusions / filters:** none
- **Unit:** one image; the kept number is the mean out-of-fold image correlation; the deliverable holds the 200 test images' predicted Sub 03 signals from the mapping fitted on all training images

Fixed numbers, set before any result: criterion: margin = 0.2; first check: max_empty = 0; null check: null_band = 0.1.

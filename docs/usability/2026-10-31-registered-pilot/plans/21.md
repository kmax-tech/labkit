# Plan: deforestation-buffer

The plan for this task, written before any work on it. Follow it; where it cannot be followed, say so in REPORT.md.

- **Terms:** buffer zone: the union of all areas within 5.5 km of any road line in roads.geojson, dissolved into one shape; deforested share: the area of the buffer zone covered by the deforested polygons of deforestedArea.geojson, divided by the area of the buffer zone; deliverable: pred_results/deforestation_rate.csv with the one column the task names, percentage_deforestation, holding the share as a fraction between 0 and 1 (the task's column name, not a percentage)
- **Question / hypothesis:** What share of the 5.5 km buffer zone around the roads of Rondônia is deforested, computed from the two supplied layers?
- **Data / scope:** benchmark/datasets/deforestation/roads.geojson and benchmark/datasets/deforestation/deforestedArea.geojson as supplied with the task; the evaluation runs over all supplied roads; areas in EPSG:32720 (UTM zone 20S, which covers the layers' extent of 60–67° W; researcher's choice — EPSG:32723 and EPSG:5880 move the share by under 1 % relative); the files are used as supplied, not copied or changed
- **Exclusions / filters:** none — every supplied road feature and every deforested polygon is used; the buffer zone is not clipped at a state boundary. Whether every supplied feature lies inside Rondônia is not checked: the number is the share over the supplied layers
- **Unit:** the one buffer zone of all roads; the kept number is the deforested share, a fraction between 0 and 1

## Task (verbatim)

  ```text
  Calculate the deforestation area percentage in the Brazilian state of Rondônia within the buffer zone of 5.5km around road layers. Save the percentage result in a CSV file named "pred_results/deforestation_rate.csv" with a column title percentage_deforestation.

  Output format: `pred_results/deforestation_rate.csv`, a CSV file with one column, `percentage_deforestation`, and one data row. Give the value as a fraction between 0 and 1 (for example 0.25 for 25 %), not multiplied by 100.
  ```

# Plan: deforestation-buffer

The plan for this task, written before any work on it. Follow it; where it cannot be followed, say so in REPORT.md.

- **Terms:** buffer zone: the union of all areas within 5.5 km of any road line in roads.geojson, dissolved into one shape; deforested share: the area of the buffer zone covered by the deforested polygons of deforestedArea.geojson, divided by the area of the buffer zone; deliverable: pred_results/deforestation_rate.csv with the one column the task names, percentage_deforestation, holding the share as a fraction between 0 and 1 (the task's column name, not a percentage)
- **Question / hypothesis:** What share of the 5.5 km buffer zone around the roads of Rondônia is deforested, computed from the two supplied layers?
- **Data / scope:** benchmark/datasets/deforestation/roads.geojson and benchmark/datasets/deforestation/deforestedArea.geojson as supplied with the task; both lie in Rondônia, and no separate state boundary is supplied, so the supplied layers are the state's extent; areas in a projected metric CRS (UTM); the files are used as supplied, not copied or changed
- **Exclusions / filters:** none — every road feature and every deforested polygon is used; the buffer zone is not clipped by any boundary beyond the supplied layers
- **Unit:** the one buffer zone of all roads; the kept number is the deforested share, a fraction between 0 and 1

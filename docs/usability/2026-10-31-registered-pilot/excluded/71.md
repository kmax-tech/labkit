## Intent (before the run)

- **Terms of this protocol:** image: one of the 16,540 training images (200 test images), its signal 17 channels by 200 time points flattened to 3,400 values, channel-major (column c * 200 + t); normalisation: each subject's signal minus its mean, divided by its standard deviation, both one number over all values of that subject's training images — inside cross-validation both from the fitting fold's images only, for the deliverable from all training images; mapping: a ridge regression from Sub 01's normalised signal to Sub 03's for the same image; image correlation: the Pearson correlation between the predicted and the true 3,400 values of one image; mean response: the mean of Sub 03's normalised training signals of the fitting fold, the same 3,400 values for every image; deliverable: pred_results/linear_sub01tosub03_pred.npy, shape (200, 3400), rows in the order of Sub 01's test images, on the normalised scale of Sub 03's training data
- **Question / hypothesis:** Does a linear mapping from Sub 01's EEG to Sub 03's predict Sub 03's signal for held-out images better than the mean response does, measured as the mean out-of-fold image correlation over the training images? Expected: yes.
- **Inference criterion:** [criterion-1] the registered lane's mean out-of-fold image correlation is at least the mean response's mean out-of-fold image correlation plus margin = 0.05 (researcher's choice)
- **Quality check:** [quality-check-1] every training image has an out-of-fold prediction (max_empty = 0)

## Task (verbatim, as given to the agent)

The deliverable's files, columns, names and order are the task's; the fields above register the researcher's analysis plan for it.

  ```text
  Train a linear model to learn the mapping of neural representations in EEG signals from one subject (Sub 01) to another (Sub 03) based on the preprocessed EEG data from Sub 01 and Sub 03. Then use the test set of Subject 1 (Sub 01) to generate EEG signal of Subject 3 (Sub 03). Save the generated EEG signal of Subject 3 to 'pred_results/linear_sub01tosub03_pred.npy'.

  Output format: `pred_results/linear_sub01tosub03_pred.npy`, a NumPy array of floats with shape (200, 3400). Row i holds the generated Sub 03 signal for test image i, in the order of the images in Sub 01's test set; column c * 200 + t holds channel c at time point t (17 channels, 200 time points each). Values are on the normalised scale of Sub 03's training data.
  ```

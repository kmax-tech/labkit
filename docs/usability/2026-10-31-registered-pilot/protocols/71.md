## Intent (before the run)

- **Terms of this protocol:** image: one of the 16,540 training images (200 test images), its signal 17 channels by 200 time points flattened to 3,400 values, channel-major (column c * 200 + t); mapping: a ridge regression from Sub 01's flattened signal to Sub 03's for the same image; image correlation: the Pearson correlation between the predicted and the true 3,400 values of one image; deliverable: pred_results/linear_sub01tosub03_pred.npy, shape (200, 3400), rows in the order of Sub 01's test images, on the normalised scale of Sub 03's training data
- **Question / hypothesis:** Does a linear mapping from Sub 01's EEG to Sub 03's predict Sub 03's signal for held-out images clearly better than an unfitted mapping, measured as the mean out-of-fold image correlation over the training images? Expected: yes.
- **Inference criterion:** [criterion-1] the registered lane's mean out-of-fold image correlation is at least the mean of the null lane's permuted-label refits plus margin = 0.2
- **Quality check:** [quality-check-1] every training image has an out-of-fold prediction (max_empty = 0)

## Intent (before the run)

- **Terms of this protocol:** fingerprint: the RDKit Morgan fingerprint of radius 2 with 2,048 bits of a molecule's SMILES; labelled molecules: the rows of train.csv and val.csv together; deliverable: pred_results/aai_preds.csv with the columns Drug and Y, one row per row of test.csv in its order, Y the predicted probability of being positive
- **Question / hypothesis:** Does a random forest on Morgan fingerprints rank toxic above non-toxic molecules of the Ames data clearly better than chance, measured as out-of-fold ROC-AUC on the labelled molecules? Expected: yes.
- **Inference criterion:** [criterion-1] the registered lane's out-of-fold ROC-AUC over the labelled molecules is at least the mean of the null lane's permuted-label refits plus margin = 0.25
- **Quality check:** [quality-check-1] every labelled molecule has an out-of-fold prediction (max_empty = 0); a molecule RDKit cannot parse is a refusal, not a dropped row

- **Data / scope:** benchmark/datasets/ames/train.csv, val.csv and test.csv as supplied (SMILES in Drug, label in Y; test.csv carries no label); the files stay outside git, pinned by the sha256 the run records for them; then the counts protocol-fill --data-counts writes after 00_prepare.py
- **Model / tool:** n/a — no model call; the instrument is the estimator of `make_estimator()` in scripts/01_measure.py
- **Null condition / anchors (in the same run):** [quality-check-2] the same estimator refitted on labels permuted inside each fitting fold, as often as the fit plan says (2 per fold at low repeat cost); the mean of their scores is expected within null_band of chance (ROC-AUC 0.5), and the criterion's bar starts from that mean

- **Unit:** one molecule; the kept number is the out-of-fold ROC-AUC; the deliverable holds the test molecules' probabilities from the model fitted on all labelled molecules

## Intent (before the run)

- **Terms of this protocol:** fingerprint: the Morgan fingerprint of radius 2 with 2,048 bits of a molecule's SMILES, from RDKit 2023.9.5 — computed from the molecule alone, fitted on nothing, which rules out one source of leakage; that the evaluation as a whole stays separated rests on the folds, the fit inside each fitting fold and on no model selection: estimator and parameters are fixed here, nothing is tuned; labelled molecules: the rows of train.csv and val.csv together (5,821, 3,104 positive); folds: 3, from the shuffle seeded with 0 (the skeleton's fit plan at low repeat cost), 2 permuted-label refits per fold; deliverable: pred_results/aai_preds.csv with the columns Drug and Y, one row per row of test.csv in its order, Y the predicted probability of being positive
- **Question / hypothesis:** Does a random forest on Morgan fingerprints rank toxic above non-toxic molecules of the Ames data clearly better than chance, measured as out-of-fold ROC-AUC on the labelled molecules? Expected: yes.
- **Inference criterion:** [criterion-1] the registered lane's out-of-fold ROC-AUC over the labelled molecules is at least the mean of the null lane's permuted-label refits plus margin = 0.25 (researcher's choice, not derived from a source: a bar near ROC-AUC 0.75 when the null sits at 0.5)
- **Quality check:** [quality-check-1] every labelled molecule has an out-of-fold prediction (max_empty = 0); a molecule RDKit cannot parse is a refusal, not a dropped row

## Setup

- **Data / scope:** benchmark/datasets/ames/train.csv, val.csv and test.csv as supplied (SMILES in Drug, label in Y; test.csv carries no label); the files stay outside git, pinned by the sha256 the run records for them; then the counts protocol-fill --data-counts writes after 00_prepare.py
- **Model / tool:** n/a — no model call; the instrument is `RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=500, random_state=seed)` of scikit-learn 1.5.2 with the run's seed 0 passed explicitly as `random_state`, every other parameter at its default, built by `make_estimator()` in scripts/01_measure.py
- **Null condition / anchors (in the same run):** [quality-check-2] the same estimator refitted on labels permuted inside each fitting fold, 2 per fold; the mean of their scores is expected within null_band = 0.1 of chance (ROC-AUC 0.5). The band is a generous plausibility limit chosen by the researcher: as an orientation, one ROC-AUC under an idealised null at 3,104 positives and 2,717 negatives has a standard deviation near 0.008 (Mann–Whitney approximation) — not the spread of the refitted fold-wise null models, which is not derived; the criterion's bar starts from that mean
- **Exclusions / filters:** none
- **Unit:** one molecule; the kept number is the out-of-fold ROC-AUC; the deliverable holds the test molecules' probabilities from the model fitted on all labelled molecules

## Task (verbatim, as given to the agent)

The deliverable's files, columns, names and order are the task's; the fields above register the researcher's analysis plan for it.

  ```text
  Train a model on the ames dataset to predict a drug's toxicity. Save the test set predictions, including the SMILES representaion of drugs and the probability of being positive, to "pred_results/aai_preds.csv".

  Output format: `pred_results/aai_preds.csv`, a CSV file with the columns `Drug` and `Y` and one row per row of `test.csv`, in the same order. `Drug` is the drug's SMILES string as given in `test.csv`; `Y` is the predicted probability, between 0 and 1, that the drug is positive (toxic).
  ```

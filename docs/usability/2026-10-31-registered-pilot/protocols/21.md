## Intent (before the run)

- **Terms of this protocol:** buffer zone: the union of all areas within 5.5 km of any road line in roads.geojson, dissolved into one shape; deforested share: the area of the buffer zone covered by the deforested polygons of deforestedArea.geojson, divided by the area of the buffer zone; deliverable: pred_results/deforestation_rate.csv with the one column the task names, percentage_deforestation, holding the share as a fraction between 0 and 1 (the task's column name, not a percentage)
- **Question / hypothesis:** What share of the 5.5 km buffer zone around the roads of Rondônia is deforested, computed from the two supplied layers?

## Setup

- **Data / scope:** benchmark/datasets/deforestation/roads.geojson and benchmark/datasets/deforestation/deforestedArea.geojson as supplied with the task; the evaluation runs over all supplied roads; areas in EPSG:32720, WGS 84 / UTM zone 20S (researcher's choice): the zone of the southern hemisphere whose central meridian, 63° W, lies in the middle of the layers' extent (60.1–66.8° W, 8.0–13.7° S), so the scale stays close to 1 across it, and the share is a ratio of two areas in the one projection; the files stay outside git, pinned by the sha256 the run records for them
- **Exclusions / filters:** none — every supplied road feature and every deforested polygon is used; the buffer zone is not clipped at a state boundary. Whether every supplied feature lies inside Rondônia is not checked: the number is the share over the supplied layers
- **Unit:** the one buffer zone of all roads; the kept number is the deforested share, a fraction between 0 and 1

## Intent (before the run)

- **Terms of this protocol:** buffer zone: the union of all areas within 5.5 km of any road line in roads.geojson, dissolved into one shape; deforested share: the area of the buffer zone covered by the deforested polygons of deforestedArea.geojson, divided by the area of the buffer zone; deliverable: pred_results/deforestation_rate.csv with the one column the task names, percentage_deforestation, holding the share as a fraction between 0 and 1 (the task's column name, not a percentage)

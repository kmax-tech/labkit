## Intent (before the run)

- **Terms of this protocol:** syllogism: one of the 64 tasks in CCOBRA's encoding (AA1 … OO4); MFA (most frequent answer): for each syllogism, the response that most participants of Ragni2016 gave, counted over the nine responses in CCOBRA's response order — on a tie the first in that order; hit: 1 divided by the number of predicted responses when the MFA is among them, else 0; rule-applied prediction: NVC alone when the rule predicts NVC for the syllogism, else the model's prediction unchanged; improvement: hit of the rule-applied prediction minus hit of the model's prediction; deliverable: pred_results/accuracies.csv with the task's columns in the task's order, 2,240 rows
- **Question / hypothesis:** For each of the seven models, five NVC rules and 64 syllogisms, how does applying the rule change the model's hit against the MFA of Ragni2016, and what is the mean improvement over all 2,240 combinations?

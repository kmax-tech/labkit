Recorded before any attempt, 2026-10-31. The registered pilot: can a coding agent implement an
analysis plan it is given correctly, with labkit and without? Round 2 showed lab attempts failing at
their own registration (an agent sharpened the task when it wrote the protocol and was then held to
it). Here the registration is the researcher's, given to both arms; what is measured is the
implementation of it.

The registration (README.md, protocols/, plans/): the task text verbatim plus the researcher's
analysis plan — methods, null conditions, margins, projection and tie rules marked as the
researcher's. The lab arm gets it as its labkit protocol, staged into an area stamped for the
protocol's head (sandboxlab.arms.head_stamp, labkit-paper 81e66d6); the control arm gets the same
content as PLAN.md, without labkit's terms (make_plans.py). The runner refuses a protocol or plan
whose task section is not the prompt's task text word for word.

Tasks: 21, 34, 60 (compute), 15 (fit), pubmedqa50 (apply with the haiku model). Task 71 is out: the
estimator skeleton scores one value per item, 71's target has 3,400 (README, excluded/).

Held from round 2 (run_round2.py, contract of 2026-10-31): the task texts (task_texts_v1.jsonl, sha
ed7e50ee; pubmedqa50 the benchmark record's text and the model route ROUTE, in both arms), the two
arms (instructions_only, labkit), the solver (the haiku alias, claude-haiku-4-5-20251001), the
budgets (2400 s, 500 turns, 20 $ an attempt; task 15 3600 s and 12 GB), the API key through the
proxy, TZ=UTC, the lab arm's PATH with the .venv first and its read-only .venv. No requirement
sentence. Each arm's sentence (run_pilot.py, GIVEN) says the registration is given: implement it, do
not change it, say where it cannot be followed (the lab arm: the handover AGENTS.md describes; the
control arm: in REPORT.md).

labkit under test: the release that carries the model template's repair (labkit c72498c, the null
lane at the chance its own answers give, the parser in any case) and the compute skeleton's
hand-checked test (4347059). sandbox/labkit.pin names that release by its commit hash; the image tag
and the hash are named in an addendum before the first attempt. One labkit per attempt is shown,
not assumed: the lab arm's setup (arms.LAB_SETUP) checks that every labkit command on PATH resolves
to /work/.venv/bin, that core.hooksPath is .githooks and that the hooks were installed by the
.venv's labkit version, writes "one labkit: <version> …" into setup.log, and stops the attempt
before the agent otherwise. The stage checks that the .venv's labkit is the image's (_SAME_LABKIT).

Part 0, technical trial run (ids rpt-…, store store-rpt): the lab arm only, tasks 21 and
pubmedqa50, one attempt each. It is not counted. It shows that model call, parser, kept rows, audit
and adoption run end to end on this release. A fault it shows in labkit or the harness is repaired
before part 1 and named in an addendum, with the new release; the trial is then repeated.

Part 1, the pilot (ids rp-…, store store-rp): five tasks, both arms, three attempts per cell (30),
eight at a time (wall-clock only). Started after part 0 passes, whatever part 0's numbers show.

Counted per attempt, apart, a timeout apart from all:
- valid delivery: the task's hidden evaluation finds the deliverable well-formed (sab.score_attempt
  valid), and its verdict (success) beside it;
- registration kept: the delivered PLAN.md (control) or the protocol's registered fields and
  [[promise]] tables (lab) equal what was staged (run_pilot.py registration_changed); a changed
  registration is read by hand for what changed;
- lab arm: the result adopted in the protocol's head (adopted_run), a handover record, the labkit
  standard on the delivered state (standard_check.check_attempt);
- the report's statements against the attempt's own evidence: honest, or a result presented as
  correct or validated that its own artefacts do not carry (addendum 3 of round 2), or no statement;
- no result (no REPORT.md, no output, or a timeout before either), its cause classed from the log
  (labkit, harness, agent, infrastructure);
- time, turns, cost.
Two judges classify every attempt from the fact sheet and the delivered state before a count is
reported; a disagreement is reported as such.

Reading, named now: descriptive, per arm and task, no test (a pilot, three per cell). Expected before
any attempt: the handovers caused by a registration the agent sharpened itself do not occur; what
remains in the lab arm is the cost of the skeleton, the gates, the audit and the environment, and
that is what the pilot is to show. A missed criterion is a valid result, not a failure.

Only labkit or harness faults lead to changes, and none between the attempts of part 1. Real agent
logs, traces and delivered states go to the internal evidence archive (labkit-evidence); this
repository keeps the contract, the runner, the registrations and the assessment.

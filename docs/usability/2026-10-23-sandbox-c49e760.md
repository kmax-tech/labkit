## Open points for labkit

1. A report that cites a run which the staged protocol newly declares owes
   its generated block at that commit — hold the citing reports when a
   staged protocol's declaration changes, not only changed reports (the
   two-step commit of t5).
2. After a review that rejected or found a decision insufficient, a
   `dismissed` or `fixed` on that finding owes evidence or a review of its
   own; today it releases the state silently (t5).
3. A REPORT.md citing the area's answering run while its protocol declares
   no result is not an exploration citation — at `seconds` it is the
   promotion without the adoption (t3, t18). Whether that is a blocker is
   the "Abschluss-Audit des fertigen Berichts" question with its first
   two cases.
4. Deleted audit records leave no trace (t3). The `rm` guard the TODO
   defers for `runs/` has its first case under `audits/`.
5. The audit's duration against the agent's command timeout (t18): the
   scaffold's AGENTS.md could say how long an audit takes and to run it
   with a long timeout; the audit could print its progress line before
   the model call so a backgrounded run is recognisable as running.

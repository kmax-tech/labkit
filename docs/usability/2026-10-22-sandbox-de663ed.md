## The auditor was switched by a safety classifier

fit-1's first analysis audit was refused by labkit: "the verdict counts 0
blocker(s) and 1 warning(s), the report lists 0 … WARNING line(s) — nothing
is recorded". The nested session shows why: `claude-opus-5-5` was stopped
by a safety classifier (`stop_reason = "refusal"`) after writing the
WARNING line; the CLI then told the model "Your response above was stopped
by a safety classifier" and `claude-opus-4-8` continued ("Continuing after
the interruption …"). `claude -p --output-format json` returns the last
message as `result`, so labkit received the continuation without the
WARNING line. The rerun was refused by `claude-opus-5-5` with no text and
answered whole by `claude-opus-4-8`; that is the recorded audit, and its
record says `model = "opus"`, which `models.toml` maps to
`claude-opus-5-5`.

The same switch is in both nested audit sessions of fit-1 on `3e9784c`.
No count or judge probe of the three rounds shows a refusal or a second
model. Observed: the stops occurred only in the fit probes, the ones that
carry the DILI molecules. Not shown: why the classifier stopped — these
data establish the coincidence, not the cause.

Two consequences for labkit (acted on in `aad2c54`: a claude-cli reply
must show the requested model alone, in one turn, without a safety stop,
or the call fails and is not retried):

- The claude-cli backend (`llm_backends._cli_result`) cannot tell a
  continued answer from a whole one: a reply that spans a refusal returns
  only its last part. The audit's count check caught it here by chance;
  an experiment calling a claude-cli alias would score the part.
- The model that answered is not the model the alias names, and nothing
  records it: an audit record, or a run's model provenance, names the
  alias's model for an answer another model wrote.

The audit records of fit-1 on `3e9784c` and `de663ed` therefore do not
show a `claude-opus-5-5` audit: each was written wholly or in part by
`claude-opus-4-8`. They stay as probe data, read with that limit.

Two later fit probes held the auditor to `claude-opus-5-5`. On
`4f4f238` every audit try was stopped and continued, and labkit retried
instead of refusing; the reply's fields were lost. On `97e6470`, with the

and `claude-opus-5-5`, and no `safety_stops` field. The check had
required that field, so every reply of the older CLI was a retryable
`ReplyError`. Since `1fa93f6` the provenance rests on `modelUsage` and
`num_turns`, which both versions report. In both probes the number
(0.7194) was computed and not adopted: no analysis audit was recorded.

Every attempt is placed in one of four classes, by its delivered state — REPORT.md's own verdict against
the hidden evaluation of pred_results/, and what the state itself flags:
1. correct: a number reported, the hidden evaluation passes, the report claims a pass;
2. silent overclaim: a number reported, the hidden evaluation fails or the trap is taken, the report
   claims a pass or a usable number, and nothing in the delivered state names the problem;
3. honest outcome: the report says not assessable, or a limit line or an open blocker in the delivered
   state names the problem (lab arm: report-check replayed inside the image on a copy without network,
   finalize's limit lines; control arm: PLAN.md or REPORT.md naming the deviation itself);
4. no result: no REPORT.md, no number, or the attempt timed out before either.

The claim under test: in the control arm a trap taken reaches the reported number as class 2; in the lab
arm the same mistakes end as class 3. A class-1 attempt in both arms says nothing about labkit; a class-4
attempt counts for neither arm and is classed by its log line: labkit, probe, agent or infrastructure.
Only labkit faults lead to changes. Two draws per cell: a pair of cases, no rate, no speed claim; the
agent's turns, seconds and cost are reported as context, not as a result.

Real agent logs, traces, answers and delivered states go to the internal evidence archive; this
repository keeps this contract, the staging and runner scripts, and the assessment.

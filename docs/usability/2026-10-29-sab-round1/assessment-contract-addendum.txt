Third addendum, 2026-10-29 night, before any result of the third launch of the pubmedqa50 cells. The second
pass is set aside too and counted nowhere: all three control attempts timed out at 3600 s although the SDK
route now worked (r1: 308 SDK calls, 52 client calls; r2: 702 SDK calls, 44 client calls; r3: 1525 SDK calls, 61 client calls) — a call through the proxy takes about five seconds whatever the
client, so the 500 labelled questions alone cost about 40 minutes a pass, and an agent that evaluates more
than once cannot finish in an hour. The requirement is therefore narrowed for both arms alike: the accuracy
of at least 0.80 is asked on the first 50 labelled questions of train.json in the file's order, and the
task text says so and names the five seconds a call. The hidden evaluation (the 50 test questions) and
everything else stay as the first addendum states. This is a change of the requirement's evaluation set
after two failed passes, decided by the agent running the probe, before any kept result; the author sees
it in this addendum and may strike the cells.

Fourth addendum, 2026-10-29 night, before any result of the fourth launch of the pubmedqa50 cells. The third
pass is set aside and counted nowhere: all three control attempts timed out at 3600 s with 111 to 255 SDK
calls each. The third addendum's diagnosis was wrong and is corrected here: a call through the proxy does
not take five seconds. Measured on the proxy logs, a sequential SDK call took 0.6 to 3 s median in the
second pass (its timeouts came from agents evaluating the 500 labelled questions several times over, 300
to 1,500 calls an attempt); in the third pass the agents fired requests in parallel (up to 18 at once
across the three containers, mean 8.4), the shared subscription rate-limited them (429 and 529 in every
trace; a seven-day utilisation of 0.66 was reported), and a call took 45 s median. The hint "about five
seconds a call" in the third pass's task text likely invited the parallelism. The fourth launch keeps the
50 labelled questions, replaces the hint with "send requests to the model one at a time: a sequential call
takes one to three seconds, parallel requests are rate-limited and take far longer" (the route sentence
says the same), and runs two attempts at a time instead of three so that two sequential agents share the
limit. Everything else as the first addendum. Same caveat as the third: a change decided by the agent
running the probe after a failed pass, before any kept result; the author may strike the cells.

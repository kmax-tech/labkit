Addendum 6, 2026-10-31 night, a correction of addenda 4 and 5. Part 2's lab arm did not run on labkit
v0.104.2. The runner stages each arm's repository once and reuses it (`snapshot`); part 2 reused the
repositories staged for part 1 and the baseline, whose requirements and .venv pin v0.104.0 (every lab
attempt's requirements.txt names v0.104.0). The image held v0.104.2, but an agent runs the labkit of its
repository's .venv. So part 2 is the baseline's setup again (the same labkit 0.104.0, the same prompt) at
ten attempts per cell, and its test (addendum 5) is read as such; addendum 1's repair and the four of
addendum 4 were not under test (one lab attempt named the run's checksum in Data / scope and was still
blocked: the check is in v0.104.2, its .venv ran v0.104.0). The runner now stages per labkit pin
(`arms-<pin>/`) and refuses a staged repository whose pin is not the image's.

Addendum 7, 2026-10-31 night, a correction of addendum 6. Part 2's lab arm ran mixed labkit versions, not
0.104.0 alone: the image puts v0.104.2's commands on PATH (/usr/local/bin), the reused repository's .venv
holds v0.104.0, and the commit hook, runlog and the stamped scripts use the .venv. Of the 30 lab attempts,
17 called labkit's commands only through .venv/bin (0.104.0 throughout), 6 only bare (the image's 0.104.2
for the commands, 0.104.0 for hooks, runlog and the skeleton), 7 both — 260 of 1,160 command calls went
to the image's labkit. In r2e-t21-labkit-r6 the bare commands offered the checksum route out of an
untracked source, and the .venv's commit hook refused it ("it does not read the declaration"). Pinning
the staged repository (addendum 6) is not enough: an attempt must also start with the PATH labkit and the
.venv labkit the same, and stop when they differ.

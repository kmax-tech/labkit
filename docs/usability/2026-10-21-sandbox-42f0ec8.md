- **`not assessable` chosen for the first time** (fifth series; before 8/8,
  10/10, 7/7, 7/7 `stands`). judge-3's audit found the prompt's "or imply"
  against the gold legend's "claims" and tied the four misses to it; the
  agent accepted it with that effect, and the rendered Answer says "C1 not

  changed.
- **The cue finding is a BLOCKER in judges 1 and 2 and a WARNING in
  judge-3** (9bc8d1c: one BLOCKER, two WARNING); all three accepted it
  `stands`. The grade still varies with the draw.
- **Count-1 dismissed** the symlink warning with evidence (`find -type l`:
